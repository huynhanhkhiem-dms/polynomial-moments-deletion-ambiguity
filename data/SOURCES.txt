Public inputs and generated dyadic construction

Data files and the original provenance records are supplied in Online Resource 1 accompanying the manuscript. The GitHub code release does not bundle the raw or generated arrays.

The main family is generated by src/predictive_family.py. Large counts are implicit except in tests/run_expanded.py. Features in those expanded cases are exactly binary64; the specified rational ridge is independently verified.

Public anchored stress tests deliberately differ from natural benchmark training.
Seed 743 selects 16 original rows per dataset. Covertype's frame is its first 2,048 provider rows.
All raw feature columns are scaled by their selected-block max absolute value, floored at one.
A fixed random sign vector forms an exact rational signed-mean projection. A stereographic circle map, rational norm-squared-one-half map and Householder transform align the first selected public query to (1/2,-1/2).
Central reflection with opposite labels produces 32 common retained rows; their label-reversed copies are also deleted.
This is an adversarial construction, not original-task accuracy evidence. All steps are in src/public_anchor.py.

Optdigits labels are 5–9 versus 0–4; Covertype is class 2 versus the rest; MAGIC uses g versus h; Spambase labels are unchanged.
Selected indices, query, scales, signs and hashes are recorded in public_anchor_manifest.json in Online Resource 1.
